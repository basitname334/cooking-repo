═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 2: TEST YOUR SITE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Visit: https://cooking-repo.onrender.com/health.php
   Should show: {"status":"ok",...}

2. Visit: https://cooking-repo.onrender.com/diagnose.php
   Check all sections for issues
   ⚠️ DELETE this file after checking!

3. Visit: https://cooking-repo.onrender.com
   Should show your homepage

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 3: CHECK RENDER LOGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Render Dashboard → Your Service → "Logs" tab
2. Look for red error messages
3. Common errors:
   - "Connection refused" = Wrong DB_HOST or DB_PORT
   - "Access denied" = Wrong DB_USER or DB_PASS
   - "Table doesn't exist" = Need to import database

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2. Wait for import to complete
3. ⚠️ DELETE import_database.php after importing!
